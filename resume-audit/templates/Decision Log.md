# Decision Log

*Whose resume: [NAME]*

Optional. One entry for every decision made on this person's resume, so the reason behind each change doesn't get lost between versions. Claude adds a line each time a decision is made. When a role comes off, the why says whether it was only to fit the page or this post, or because the person doesn't want it shown anywhere. The LinkedIn playbook reads it to know whether the role can stay on LinkedIn.
